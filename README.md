**Expenses Calculator**
- A simple expenses calculator that allows you to keep track of your monthly/yearly spending and saving. 

**Key Features**
- Manually input your expenses in the excel sheet (can setup a widget on your phone for easy access).
- Automatically calculates your total expenses and savings and sends automated email at the end of the month with a PDF report.
- Email body text is color coated based on your amount saved for the month (green, yellow, orange, red)
- Sends automated email at the end of the year with your total yearly expenses and savings.
